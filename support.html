Support

Help with Fika Roulette for iPhone.

Common questions

  • I don't get the daily reminder. Open Settings in the app and check that Daily reminder is on. If the app says notifications are off, tap Open Settings and allow notifications for Fika Roulette. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.
  • The play button is silent or missing. The app uses your iPhone's built-in Swedish voice. Check the volume, and make sure a Swedish voice is available under iOS Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.
  • Why is my card the same as my friend's? Everyone gets the same card each day, so you can talk about it. "Spin again" gives you up to two extra cards of your own.
  • How do I add the widget? Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Fika Roulette.
  • How do I start over? Settings › Reset streak clears your streak and calendar. Saved cards stay.
