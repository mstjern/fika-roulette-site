Fika Roulette – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version: Fika Roulette does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app does with your data

Nothing leaves your phone. Fika Roulette works fully offline and makes no network requests. All 365 cards are included in the app itself, and the audio is spoken on your iPhone by its built-in Swedish voice.

What is stored on your device

To remember your day-to-day progress, the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • your streak and the days you have taken a moment;
  • the cards you have saved;
  • your settings, such as whether the daily reminder is on and at what time.

This stays on your device and is not sent to me or to anyone else. If you delete the app, it is deleted too.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled by your phone. It is not sent from a server, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Widget and sharing

The home-screen widget shows a card from the app's built-in content. When you share a card as an image, it goes only where you choose to send it, using the standard iOS share sheet.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code, advertising or analytics, so no third party receives any information from it.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it is safe for the whole family to use.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
